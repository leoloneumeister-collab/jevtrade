Jev Stock Check

Ask Jev for its read on a stock, then check whether its past reads were any good.

Paper only. Not financial advice. Jev has no proven edge on stocks. Most days the honest answer is "sit out". This app cannot place trades.

Watchlist scan

Ask Jev about many tickers at once, strongest first. One ticker proves nothing, so look at the spread. Max 25.

Cross-stock ranking test

The fairest test of Jev. Each day it rates every stock, we rank them, then compare the top fifth with the bottom fifth. Market drift and Jev's overall mood cancel out. Needs at least 5 stocks. Leave tickers empty to use every CSV in your data folder (or the default list for live prices).

Forward scoreboard

Every live read is saved (file and demo reads never are). After the look-ahead period passes, it is checked against what the price did. This is the only test that cannot be fooled by old data.
